Why Annuncia

Most screen recorders want your recordings in their cloud. Annuncia records your screen, voice and face, cleans it up and transcribes it on your Mac, and gives you an MP4. Here’s how it compares with Loom, BetterCapture and the other recorders people ask about.

What you get that the others charge for, or upload.

Nothing to sign up for, nothing uploaded.

Loom and Tella keep your recordings in their cloud, behind an account. Annuncia has no account. It saves an MP4 to a folder you choose, and the recording and its transcript never leave your Mac.

Free, with no cap.

Loom’s free plan stops at 25 videos of five minutes each, at 720p, with no download. Cap’s free version is for personal use only, and Tella has no free plan. Annuncia is free and MIT licensed, with no limit on length, count or resolution.

Clean up and transcripts, on your Mac.

Cutting filler words and silences is a Business + AI feature in Loom, and Tella does it in its cloud. Annuncia does it in one click, as cuts you can see and undo, and transcribes on your Mac’s GPU. Neither costs anything or sends your voice anywhere.

Send the file, not a link.

When you stop, Copy puts the MP4 itself on your clipboard. Paste it into Slack, Mail or Messages, and whoever gets it can watch it without an account, a browser tab or a plan.

When something else is the better pick.

Annuncia does one job: record on your Mac and send a file. If you need more than that, these do it well.

Share links for a team

If your team watches recordings from links and keeps them in a shared library, Loom, Tella and Cap Pro do that today. Annuncia Cloud will add optional links and team libraries, but it isn’t here yet.

Polished product demos

For marketing videos with automatic zooms and backgrounds, look at Screen Studio, CleanShot X or Cap’s Studio Mode. Annuncia records what’s on your screen as it is.

Pro formats, or not a Mac

BetterCapture is free, open source and native too, and records ProRes, HDR and alpha. If you aren’t on a Mac, Cap runs on Windows and Linux, and Loom on Windows and in Chrome.
