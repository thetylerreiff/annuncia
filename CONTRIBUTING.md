# Contributing

Annuncia is open source under the [MIT License](LICENSE), but it isn't taking pull requests yet while the app finds its shape. You're welcome to fork it and change it however you like.

Bug reports and ideas are welcome as [issues](https://github.com/thetylerreiff/annuncia/issues). For a bug, include your macOS version, your Mac, and the steps that lead to it.

To report a security problem, use [private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/thetylerreiff/annuncia/security/advisories/new) rather than a public issue.
